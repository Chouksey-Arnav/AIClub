BEFORE YOU JOIN

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to know how to code already?+
No. Sessions start from the basics of building an app. If you already code, you'll move faster and can jump into the advanced track sooner.
Do I need my own laptop?+
Yes — bring a laptop to sessions. Everything you build lives on it (and eventually, on a live server you control).
Is there a cost?+
AI Forge itself is free to join. Some tools used during the pipeline (like a domain name) have small real-world costs — we'll walk through the cheapest options together.
When do sessions actually meet?+
AI Forge is finishing up official chartering at Green Level HS. Exact meeting days and times are being finalized — join the list below and you'll be the first to know.
What if I only care about the AI part, not the servers?+
Totally fine to stay focused there — but part of the point of AI Forge is that AI is only useful once it's actually live in something people can use. The full pipeline is what makes that possible.
